# DOOM portfolio player notices

## DOOM Shareware v1.9

This player includes the original, unmodified DOS shareware release of DOOM
v1.9 (Episode One: Knee-Deep in the Dead). `DOOM.EXE`, `DOOM1.WAD`, and the
accompanying files were extracted from id Software's `doom19s.zip`; no
registered or commercial DOOM IWAD is included.

The bundle also includes a generated `DEFAULT.CFG` that selects DOOM's
Sound Blaster/AdLib OPL music device. The music itself remains the original
MUS data embedded in the unmodified `DOOM1.WAD`; no replacement soundtrack
or external music files are included.
